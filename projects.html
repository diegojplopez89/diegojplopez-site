Humanitarian operations

Casa Alitas shelter response

Diego's work at Casa Alitas developed alongside the growth of a community-based shelter response for asylum-seeking families in southern Arizona. Independent reporting documents rapid changes in daily arrivals, expansion into larger facilities, coordination with local and federal partners, and the challenge of maintaining safe reception capacity during funding uncertainty.

Migration systems

Referral and coordination infrastructure

Professional work has included improving the way border shelters and interior service organizations share information and connect newly arrived asylum seekers with resources after they reach destination communities. The emphasis is on practical referral workflows, language access, continuity of support, and reducing information gaps between organizations.

Research has explored how migration policy intersects with health, including the needs of Indigenous asylum seekers, language access, COVID-19, trauma-informed care, and community-based humanitarian response.
