Diego Javier Piña Lopez, LMSW, MPA

For more than a decade, my work has centered on people moving through high-stress systems and on the organizations trying to serve them well. I have led large humanitarian operations, built partnerships across government and nonprofit organizations, designed practical data and referral systems, contributed to public-health research, and now practice as a Licensed Master Social Worker in Arizona.

What I bring

Leadership that connects people, operations, and systems

I do not see leadership, direct service, and systems work as separate things. The best work happens when the people making decisions understand what is happening on the ground, when teams know what they are responsible for, and when the tools around them make the work easier instead of harder.

Leadership & operations

Lead through complexity

At Casa Alitas, I helped lead a rapidly changing, multi-site humanitarian response that depended on staff, volunteers, government partners, nonprofits, funders, transportation, data, and community trust all working together.

Transformation & implementation

Build what the work actually needs

Many of the systems I have built started with a practical problem: people needed a clearer workflow, better information, or a way to coordinate across organizations. My approach is to understand the work first, then build the simplest useful structure around it.

Clinical & public health

Keep the person in view

My LMSW and clinical practice add a frontline perspective to the broader work. They keep questions of trauma, access, family systems, and service design connected to the experience of the person actually moving through the system.

Research & evidence
