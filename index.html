A place to begin again

A shelter can be more than a bed for the night.

At Casa Alitas, the work was to create a place of transition: a welcoming, trauma-informed environment where people arriving from government custody could rest, reconnect with family, and begin to feel like themselves again.

That belief still guides how I approach leadership, direct service, research, and technology: every system should make it easier for someone to take their next step.
