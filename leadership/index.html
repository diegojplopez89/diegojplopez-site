Catholic Community Services / Casa Alitas · Executive Director, Migrant Services · 2019–2024

Executive leadership of a high-volume multi-site humanitarian operation

Directed a $12 million annual multi-site humanitarian reception system serving up to 1,800 asylum seekers nightly, leading 62 staff and more than 600 volunteers. Responsibilities included operations, staffing, logistics, partnerships, public funding, risk, data systems, community engagement, media communication, and rapid adaptation to federal-policy and arrival-volume changes.

Independent reporting documents Diego's progression from lead coordinator during the 2019 expansion into the Benedictine Monastery to program manager and later program director as the system grew and adapted.
