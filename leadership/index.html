Experience & Leadership

I learned leadership by moving through the work itself

Catholic Community Services / Casa Alitas · Leadership progression · 2019–2024

From program coordination to Executive Director

I progressed through Program Coordinator, director-level leadership, and ultimately Executive Director in 2023. At the executive level, I led a $12 million annual multi-site humanitarian reception system serving up to 1,800 asylum seekers nightly with 62 staff and more than 600 volunteers. The work included staffing, logistics, transportation, funding, risk, data systems, community engagement, media communication, partnerships, and adapting quickly when policy or arrival patterns changed.

What I value most from that experience is not only the scale. It is the relationships and trust that made the scale possible. County and federal partners, nonprofits, faith communities, volunteers, funders, staff, and the people we were receiving all had to be part of the same response.
