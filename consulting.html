Consulting & training

Support for teams doing work that matters.

I work with nonprofits, public agencies, humanitarian organizations, and community partners facing complex operational, organizational, and human challenges.

01

Humanitarian response & program design

Build or strengthen reception, shelter, referral, and community-based support programs that treat dignity as an operational requirement.

  • Emergency shelter and reception planning
  • Transition from custody to community
  • Migration and refugee-support strategy
  • Community and interagency coordination
02

Trauma-informed leadership & training

Help teams respond thoughtfully to people in crisis while supporting the staff and volunteers doing demanding frontline work.

  • Psychological first aid training
  • Trauma-informed environments and communication
  • Staff and volunteer readiness
  • Leadership under pressure and change
03

Nonprofit leadership & governance

Strengthen mission-driven organizations through clearer strategy, healthier partnerships, stronger governance, and practical operational planning.

  • Organizational development and strategic planning
  • Board roles, bylaws, and governance practices
  • Volunteer engagement and coalition building
  • Funding strategy and program sustainability
04

Research, data & human-centered systems

Make information more useful to the people responsible for coordinating services, understanding outcomes, and making decisions.

  • Referral networks and case-management workflows
  • Dashboards and operational reporting
  • Community needs and service-access assessment
  • Practical technology for human-service teams

How I work

Start with people. Stay close to reality.

I am interested in solutions that make sense to the people who will actually use them. That means listening to frontline teams, understanding the organizational constraints, building trust across partners, and translating big goals into workable next steps.

Engagements can include focused consultations, training sessions, project-based support, speaking, or longer-term collaboration depending on the need.
