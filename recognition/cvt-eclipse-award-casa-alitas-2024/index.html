Casa Alitas receives CVT's 2024 Eclipse Award

Humanitarian recognitionCasa AlitasCenter for Victims of TortureProfessional service

The Center for Victims of Torture presented its 2024 Eclipse Award to Casa Alitas in recognition of the welcome center's humanitarian work with people seeking asylum and safety in the United States.

CVT's public record states that Diego Piña Lopez, identified as the former director of Casa Alitas, accepted the award from CVT President and CEO Simon Adams. CVT also highlighted Diego's participation in its 2024 Courageous Voices event, a public conversation connected to the International Day in Support of Victims of Torture.

This page intentionally distinguishes organizational recognition from personal recognition. The Eclipse Award was presented to Casa Alitas, not to Diego individually. Diego's relevance to the record is that CVT independently identifies him as the former director who accepted the award on behalf of the organization.
