Humanitarian recognition · 2024

Casa Alitas receives the Center for Victims of Torture Eclipse Award

The Center for Victims of Torture presented its 2024 Eclipse Award to Casa Alitas for its work supporting families seeking asylum and safety in the United States. CVT's June 26 coverage states that Diego, as former director of Casa Alitas, accepted the award from CVT President and CEO Simon Adams. This was an organizational award to Casa Alitas, not an individual award to Diego.
