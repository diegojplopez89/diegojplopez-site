Therapy & support

You deserve support that meets you where you are.

Life can ask a lot of you—especially when you are navigating parenthood, major transitions, anxiety, trauma, or the feeling that your experience is not fully seen. You do not have to sort through it alone.

Diego Piña López · LMSW · Tucson, Arizona

A real conversation. Care built around your life.

My clinical approach is grounded in the same belief that guides the rest of my work: people deserve to be understood in the context of their own lives, cultures, relationships, strengths, and experiences.

I work with people ages eight and older, including children, teenagers, adults, and families. Together, we can build practical support that reflects what matters to you—without asking you to fit a mold.

As an Arizona-licensed master social worker, I provide clinical services under appropriate supervision. In-person and online appointments are available through my clinical practice.

My approach

Support shaped around your story—not a script.

I use a collaborative, person-centered, and strengths-based approach. Depending on your goals, our work may include trauma-informed care, culturally responsive practices, cognitive behavioral strategies, dialectical behavioral tools, or other integrative approaches.

The goal is not to squeeze your life into a formula. It is to help you feel heard, find tools that make sense for your situation, and move forward with more confidence and less isolation.
