Biography

Diego Javier Piña Lopez, LMSW, MPA

Humanitarian and nonprofit leader, Licensed Master Social Worker, researcher, board member, and systems builder whose work spans crisis operations, migration, program implementation, public health, clinical social work, partnerships, governance, and technology-enabled coordination.

Leadership built from the ground up

Diego's career developed from frontline and volunteer service into program coordination, director-level responsibilities, and executive leadership. At Casa Alitas, he ultimately directed a $12 million multi-site humanitarian reception system serving up to 1,800 asylum seekers nightly with 62 staff and more than 600 volunteers.

That experience required more than shelter management: it combined staffing, logistics, public funding, risk, transportation, data, media communication, cross-sector partnerships, volunteer mobilization, and rapid adaptation to changes in federal policy and arrival volume.

Transformation, implementation, and systems

Across Catholic Charities USA, Cherokee Federal, and related work, Diego has translated complex service problems into referral workflows, dashboards, reporting structures, shared data practices, documentation, training, and decision-support tools. His professional interest in systems now also includes local-first AI, workflow automation, knowledge management, and responsible human approval gates.

LMSW and public-health perspective

Psychology Today and Amanah Bloom currently identify Diego Piña Lopez, LMSW, in clinical social work practice in Tucson. The credential adds direct-practice depth to a broader leadership profile: decisions about policy, operations, and technology are informed by firsthand understanding of people, families, trauma, access, and service delivery.
