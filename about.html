Biography

Diego Javier Piña Lopez, MSW, MPA

Humanitarian leader, social worker, researcher, nonprofit strategist, and board member whose work has focused on migration, asylum response, public health, organizational leadership, and practical systems for communities navigating crisis.

Humanitarian leadership and migration

Diego's professional work has centered on helping organizations respond to migration with dignity, coordination, and practical support. Independent reporting documents his progression through multiple Casa Alitas leadership roles as Tucson's humanitarian reception system expanded and adapted to major changes in arrival volume and federal policy.

His work has included shelter operations, cross-agency coordination, humanitarian reception, public communication, and systems that help newly arrived families connect with sponsors and communities across the United States. See the source-linked Leadership & Service record and Press & Media archive.

Research and public health

Diego co-authored the peer-reviewed article Health Sciences Interprofessional Collaborative: A Perspective on Migration, COVID-19, and the Impact on Indigenous Communities, published in Frontiers in Sociology in 2021. A University of Arizona faculty record also lists him as a co-author on a 2025 American Association of Geographers presentation concerning asylum politics and local humanitarianisms along the U.S.-Mexico border.
