AZ Luminaria · February 29, 2024

Independent reporting on Casa Alitas leadership at scale

Humanitarian leadershipCasa AlitasOperationsVolunteer coordinationTucson

AZ Luminaria published an in-depth report from inside the Casa Alitas Welcome Center during a period of intense operational and funding pressure. The story identifies Diego Lopez—Diego Javier Piña Lopez—as director of Casa Alitas and documents the scale of the organization he was leading.

The reporting contrasts the program's earlier scale with its later operations. It notes that in 2019 Diego was the shelter's only paid staff member when Casa Alitas operated from a small house and served roughly 30 people per day. By early 2024, the report described a staff of approximately 40–50 people, a volunteer corps approaching 900, several operating locations, and a large central facility coordinating intake, food, medical support, shelter, transportation, and departures.

The story also documents the practical demands of high-volume humanitarian operations. Casa Alitas was receiving hundreds and at times more than 1,000 people in a day, while staff and volunteers coordinated basic needs, medical services, communications, travel support, and safe short-term lodging.

For Diego's professional record, the significance of this coverage is not simply the size of the shelter. It independently illustrates organizational growth, frontline executive decision-making, volunteer and staff coordination, rapid adaptation, public communication, and the challenge of maintaining humane services while funding and arrival patterns changed quickly.
