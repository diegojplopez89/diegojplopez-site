KJZZ · February 21, 2024

Maintaining humanitarian services during a major funding shortfall

MigrationHumanitarian responseCasa AlitasOperations

KJZZ reported that the Pima County migrant-care network was preparing for a severe funding shortfall while Casa Alitas continued receiving people released after federal immigration processing.

Diego Piña Lopez described the difficulty of sustaining high-volume humanitarian work without the federal funding that had supported shelter operations. The story also documented layoffs and efforts to identify a smaller set of services that could still be maintained.

His comments centered on the immediate humanitarian objective: reducing suffering and determining how many families could continue to receive basic care. The article provides a useful snapshot of the operational tradeoffs that occur when humanitarian demand remains high while resources contract.
