Cronkite News · September 26, 2023

Coordinating shelter response during a rapid increase in arrivals

MigrationHumanitarian responseCasa AlitasCoordination

Cronkite News reported on a surge in migrant encounters in the Tucson sector and the strain that rapidly changing arrival patterns placed on government agencies and humanitarian organizations.

Diego Piña Lopez described how quickly conditions could change for shelters and estimated that Casa Alitas had assisted tens of thousands of people during a particularly intense two-month period. He emphasized the importance of organizations changing practices together rather than operating in isolation.

The coverage captures a central theme of Diego's shelter work: large-scale response depends on coordination across organizations, shared practices, and the ability to adapt operations quickly as arrival patterns change.
