AZPM · March 1, 2024

Community response as federal shelter funding approached a cliff

MigrationHumanitarian responseCasa Alitas

Arizona Public Media reported on the funding pressure facing Casa Alitas and other local partners as federal support for migrant services approached exhaustion in early 2024.

In the report, Diego Piña Lopez, then program director of Casa Alitas, explained the practical cost of meeting basic needs such as food, clothing, and temporary shelter. He also described the possibility that a loss of reception capacity could leave families and very young children without a safe place to go after release.

The coverage illustrates a recurring operational challenge in humanitarian response: translating large policy and funding decisions into immediate decisions about beds, meals, transportation, staffing, and which families can be served first. Diego emphasized community resources and prioritizing the most vulnerable families.
