Curriculum Vitae

Diego Javier Piña Lopez, LMSW, MPA

For more than a decade, my work has centered on people moving through high-stress systems and on the organizations trying to serve them well. That has taken me from frontline humanitarian work to executive leadership, national research and planning, clinical social work, board service, and the design of practical data and referral systems.

Focused resumes

Professional focus

I work best where mission, operations, people, and systems meet. My experience includes large-scale humanitarian response, organizational leadership, public administration, program implementation, government and nonprofit partnerships, public health, clinical social work, research, governance, and technology-enabled coordination.

Professional experience

2023–2025 · Remote / Arizona

Researcher & Content Expert — Cherokee Federal

Produced operational reports and dashboards for government and humanitarian decision-makers; assessed shelter, medical, and community capacity; consulted with service providers on referral and settlement systems; and managed a 10-person case-management team supporting refugee employment partnerships in Arizona.

2019–2024 · Tucson, Arizona

Catholic Community Services / Casa Alitas — Program Coordinator → Director → Executive Director (2023)

Progressed from program coordination and director-level roles into executive leadership. At the executive level, led a $12 million multi-site humanitarian reception system serving up to 1,800 asylum seekers nightly with 62 staff and more than 600 volunteers. The work combined staffing, logistics, partnerships, public funding, risk, data, community engagement, media communication, and rapid adaptation to changing arrival patterns and policy.

2022–2024 · Remote / National

Consultant — Catholic Charities USA

Supported multi-agency referral, data, and reporting systems connecting border asylum-service programs with interior case-management partners. Work included shared workflows, common data fields, implementation support, partner coordination, and high-volume migration-service data.

2021–2022 · U.S.–Mexico Border Region

Senior Border Specialist — Save the Children

Managed border-response programs and partnerships, contributed to planning and allocation within a $7 million response portfolio, and supported monitoring, coordination, and implementation across partner organizations.

Clinical social work

I am a Licensed Master Social Worker in Arizona. Current public professional listings through Psychology Today and Amanah Bloom identify me in clinical practice in Tucson. I see that work as connected to, rather than separate from, my broader career: direct practice keeps questions of trauma, access, family systems, and service design grounded in the experience of the person actually moving through the system.

Selected systems work

My systems work grew out of operational necessity. When enterprise tools were not available, I built practical workflows using accessible technology; later work expanded into multi-agency data systems, referral interoperability, dashboards, reporting structures, and local-first AI and automation experiments. I am interested in technology when it makes work clearer, faster, or more humane—not simply because it is new.
